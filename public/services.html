Services catalog

Every service has a stable task_type identifier. Use it in the task form or in a POST /api/v1/tasks request. Every service is free during the proof-of-concept pilot — see pricing for what happens after.

task_type: real_world_verification

Real-world verification

Confirm that a place, product, price, business, or claim exists and matches its description. Evidence included: photos, receipts, timestamps, written observations.

Examples: "Is this product on the shelf at this store?" · "Does this office actually exist at this address?" · "Is the posted price accurate today?"

Free during pilot · output: text_report_with_photos
task_type: product_or_app_testing

Product or app testing

Install, use, and test software, apps, or physical products as a real human user. Honest report of friction, bugs, confusion, and first impressions.

Examples: "Complete signup and first purchase, note every hesitation." · "Test this flow on a real Android device." · "Unbox and assemble the product, time it."

Free during pilot · output: annotated_screenshots / video
task_type: human_judgment_and_feedback

Human judgment and feedback

Structured subjective evaluation a model can't safely make alone: tone, clarity, trustworthiness, design quality, cultural fit, plausibility.

Examples: "Does this landing page feel trustworthy to a native speaker of your target market?" · "Rank these five headlines for credibility." · "Would a human find this email pushy?"

Free during pilot · output: structured_json / text_report
task_type: data_collection

Data collection

Gather, label, or verify data that needs human perception, local access, or manual research — the data no crawler can reach.

Examples: "Collect opening hours for these 20 shops by calling them." · "Photograph street signage in this neighborhood." · "Label these 200 images for ambiguity."

Free during pilot · output: structured_json / spreadsheet
task_type: local_physical_task

Local physical-world tasks

Errands and on-location actions in the operator's service area: visit, photograph, deliver, check, measure, observe, sign for. The hands your agent doesn't have.

Examples: "Photograph the queue at this location at 09:00." · "Pick up and re-ship a package." · "Measure the storefront and note foot traffic."

Free during pilot · output: text_report_with_photos
task_type: ai_output_review

AI output review

A human checkpoint for AI-generated text, code, images, or decisions before they reach production or end users. Catch what evaluation metrics miss.

Examples: "Review 50 AI-written support replies for tone failures." · "Sanity-check this generated contract summary." · "Flag hallucinated facts in these articles."

Free during pilot · output: structured_json / annotated_report
task_type: prompt_and_workflow_testing

Prompt and workflow testing

Run your prompts, agent flows, or automations as a deliberate human tester. Report failure modes, confusing branches, and edge cases a happy-path test misses.

Examples: "Try to break this chatbot in 30 minutes." · "Follow the agent's instructions literally and log where they fail." · "Test this prompt with adversarial inputs."

Free during pilot · output: text_report / structured_json
task_type: simulation_and_automation_testing

Simulation and automation testing

Act as the human inside a simulated scenario: play the customer, follow the script, receive the call, complete the checkout — and verify the pipeline end to end.

Examples: "Be the test customer for our voice agent, three scenarios." · "Complete a real order through the automated flow and confirm every email arrives."

Free during pilot · output: text_report / video
task_type: accessibility_and_usability_check

Accessibility and usability check

A human pass over your product for readability, navigation, accessibility barriers, and first-use confusion — the things automated audits score but don't feel.

Examples: "Navigate the site keyboard-only and report blockers." · "Is this readable on a phone in sunlight?" · "First-time-user walkthrough, spoken aloud."

Free during pilot · output: annotated_report
task_type: custom_human_in_the_loop

Custom human-in-the-loop tasks

Any recurring or one-off checkpoint where an autonomous system needs a human decision, signature, or observation. Describe the loop; we design the human step. If your task fits no category above, this is the one to use.

Examples: "Approve high-risk agent actions within 4 hours." · "Daily human spot-check of automated listings." · "Human escalation contact for our support bot."

Free during pilot · output: as agreed

Don't see your task?

The catalog is examples, not limits. Anything a human can legally and safely do is in scope — these ten types exist so agents can index the offer, not to fence it. Submit an unlisted need as custom_human_in_the_loop and describe it plainly, or ask the operator first. Every request gets a human answer at review, and recurring unlisted requests become new catalog entries.
