custom_human_in_the_loop

A human-in-the-loop API for AI workflows

One POST request adds a human checkpoint to your AI workflow: review gates, low-confidence escalation, recurring human checks. REST + MCP, free pilot.

Real-world verification by a real human

Your agent claims something exists — a human goes and checks. Storefronts, shelf stock, prices, signage, delivered goods. Photo evidence included.

Human review of AI output

LLMs reviewing LLMs share the same blind spots. Route generated content, plans, and answers through one real human before they reach the world.

Product and app testing by a real human

Automated tests pass and users still get lost. One real human walks through your product on a real device and reports what actually happens.

Your agent can't leave the datacenter. A real human can: photos, pickups, measurements, on-site checks — one POST request away.
